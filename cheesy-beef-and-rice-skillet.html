Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup uncooked long-grain white rice
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 2 cups low-sodium beef broth
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef and onion in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat for 6–8 minutes, breaking up the beef. Cook ground beef to 160°F and drain excess fat.
  2. Stir in garlic and paprika for 30 seconds. Add rice, tomatoes, broth, salt and pepper.
  3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 18–20 minutes or until rice is tender. Add a splash of water if needed.
  4. Remove from heat, sprinkle with cheddar, cover for 2–3 minutes until melted, and serve.

Budget tip

Use store-brand rice and cheese, or replace half the beef with drained canned beans.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate leftovers in a shallow airtight container within 2 hours. Use within 3–4 days and reheat to 165°F.
