HOW TO PREVIEW:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in a browser.

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Replace decorative emoji/gradient image placeholders with original or properly licensed food photography.
2. Test and verify all recipes, quantities, cook times and food-safety instructions.
3. Create your actual Payhip products and replace placeholder links in shop.html with the individual product URLs.
4. Add a real business contact email.
5. Review privacy, cookie, consumer and affiliate disclosure requirements applicable to your location and tools.
6. Confirm your affiliate program permits Pinterest traffic and direct linking before adding affiliate links.
7. Update product availability and prices if they change.
