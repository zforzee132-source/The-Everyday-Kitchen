Lemon Garlic Chicken & Broccoli

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 25 min · Total: 35 min (estimated) · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 lb broccoli florets
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Pat chicken dry and pound to an even ¾-inch thickness.
  2. Mix 2 tbsp oil, lemon zest and juice, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper. Coat chicken and arrange on a rimmed sheet pan.
  3. Roast for 10 minutes. Toss broccoli with remaining oil and add to the pan in a single layer.
  4. Roast another 12–15 minutes, until broccoli is tender and chicken reaches 165°F in its thickest part. Continue cooking if needed.
  5. Rest chicken for 5 minutes before slicing. Serve with broccoli and optional rice.

Budget tip

Buy chicken in family packs and freeze portions; use frozen broccoli if cheaper (roast separately if needed).

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate leftovers in a shallow airtight container within 2 hours. Use within 3–4 days and reheat to 165°F.
