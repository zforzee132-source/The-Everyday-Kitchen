Build-Your-Own Taco Bowls

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 20 min · Total: 30 min (estimated) · Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dry long-grain white rice
  • Water according to rice package
  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  • ¼ cup water for beef
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup salsa or pico de gallo
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional)
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Instructions

  1. Cook rice according to package directions while preparing toppings.
  2. Brown beef in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into crumbles, until it reaches 160°F. Drain excess fat.
  3. Add taco seasoning and ¼ cup water; simmer 2–3 minutes.
  4. Warm beans and corn in a separate pan or microwave until hot.
  5. Divide rice, beef, beans and corn among four bowls. Top with lettuce, salsa, cheese, optional avocado and lime.

Budget tip

Replace half the beef with extra beans and skip avocado when expensive.

Storage and reheating

Refrigerate rice and beef promptly in shallow containers within 2 hours; keep cold toppings separate. Use within 3–4 days and reheat hot components to 165°F.
